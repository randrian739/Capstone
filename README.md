# Capstone
Repository for potential Capstone ideas

Idea 1 Available Dog Parks

This project will consist of the available dog parks in the users area on a map (may be limited to NJ) and show a rating from legitimate dog owners who have attended said park. This is good for users who are new to an area whether they are new owners or just visiting.

Idea 2 Public/State Owned Fishing Spots

There lakes and bodies of water across the US, however, some may mot available to the public for fishing or have no fish at all. This project will help the user determine accessible fishing spots without the worry of trespassing on private property or wasting time fishing in a lake with no fish.

Idea 3 Arcades In Your Area

If the user loves games and going out, this project help said users find available public gaming/entertainment centers to enjoy with their friends and family. It can range from VR/AR to Arcade places to even places like Top Golf.
